# parché principles

- **No brand favouritism.** parché never favours one brand over another: no brand spotlights, no local or origin labelling, no extra weighting, caps or placement for any label. Every brand competes on the same rules in packing lists, looks and shopping options.
- **The client comes first.** Every suggestion is curated, conscious and fashion-aware: chosen for the traveller's trip, style and climate, never for a brand relationship. Affiliate links may change where a click goes, never what is recommended.
