# Verified product comparison pilot

Six exact retailer products reviewed on 2026-10-22: three cotton tees per womenswear/menswear selection. Each catalogue entry stores the product page, its own merchant image, listed price and currency, and review date. This is a deliberately narrow first category, not a complete shoppable wardrobe.

## Decisions
- Preserve the existing sand/white palette and restrained cards. Present three equal alternatives with no budget question or price filter; stack on mobile.
- Merchant product images use contain to avoid cropping. Failed photos show an honest placeholder, never unrelated stock imagery.
- Outfit/editorial photos remain separate and labelled as inspiration.
- Remove AI product generation and product Unsplash searches. Shopping uses zero paid service calls and requires no new credentials.
- Display original USD/CAD currencies, review dates and retailer availability checks; no live stock or price guarantee. Hide snapshots after 30 days until reviewed.
- Direct retailer links only. Affiliate agreements, approved image feeds and tracking IDs still need configuration before monetization. No commission claim.

## Maintenance and expansion
Review each exact colour/product URL, merchant image, price, currency, availability and retailer conditions before updating checkedAt. Add trousers, footwear, outerwear and accessories as coherent three-tier groups, then add climate/activity selection rules. Do not fabricate missing options. Before production rollout, obtain approved affiliate product feeds/image usage terms and verify shipping coverage for the target market.

## Verification
Unit coverage checks all tiers, distinct image/product mappings, snapshot expiry, trip validation and no paid calls. Build/type checks plus preview browser verification cover integration. Product availability remains retailer controlled.
